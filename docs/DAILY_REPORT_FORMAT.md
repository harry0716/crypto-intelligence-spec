# Daily Report Format

The daily report is designed for a beginner who needs to understand what is
known, what is only reported, and where to investigate next.

## Reading order

1. **Today's conclusions**: a short Traditional Chinese orientation. It states
   market movement, event count, and how many items are still pending
   corroboration.
2. **Market thermometer**: BTC/USD, USDT/USD, BTC dominance, and a plain
   language reading. These are observations, not forecasts.
3. **Source Radar**: the newest item available from each source. Original
   headlines and source summaries are preserved, together with the direct link,
   tier, source type, claim boundary, verification state, and a follow-up step.
4. **Today's key events**: ranked events with the original summary and a short
   Traditional Chinese explanation for a beginner.
5. **Learning corner**: definitions that help a new reader interpret the
   report.
6. **Quality and risk**: source diversity, official-primary count, number of
   unconfirmed items, and collection warnings.
7. **24-72 hour watchlist**: conditions to check next, rather than a price
   prediction.

## Evidence labels

- **Primary source**: a T1 official first-party record.
- **Issuer statement**: a T2 project's own announcement.
- **Pending corroboration**: a T3 or T4 item that must not be treated as a
  confirmed fact or as standalone trading evidence.

Original source titles, snippets, timestamps, and URLs stay in their original
language so a reader can verify the context without relying on a translation.
